Before You Start... 👋

quick cheat sheet so you don't get lost! I wish someone gave ME one of these before I started reading lol

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 who's who

  • Vafiya, 19 — gentle, funny, braver than she looks. lost her mum two months before the story starts, got the same weak heart. wears her mum's blue scarf, hums her lullaby, writes letters she can never send.
  • Zayn, 20 — quiet, careful, kind. fixes watches in his late grandfather's shop. left ear already gone, right ear fading fast. used to play guitar. stopped.
  • Khala Sabina — Vafiya's aunt. warm, practical, hides her fear behind food (always).
  • Chacha Rahim — runs the tea stall by the park. sees everything. says almost nothing.
  • Imran — Zayn's loud, loyal best friend. makes everyone laugh, about 90% of the time.
  • Dr. Meher — Vafiya's heart doctor (a cardiologist). honest, gentle, secretly my favorite side character.
  • Noor — a small girl who shows up near the very end. just wait.

🔑 motifs to watch for

  • The Bench — where everything starts AND ends. the loose slat is their secret mailbox.
  • 6:40 — when Zayn's grandfather's watch stopped. dawn 6:40 = Zayn reads. dusk 6:40 = Vafiya writes. the one minute where Morning and Dusk actually meet.
  • Three taps — on the wrist, on glass, wherever. means "I'm here." this one will wreck you by the end, I promise.
  • The Blue Scarf — her mum's love, always close by.
  • The Lullaby — the tune she hums. he hears it before he even knows her.
  • Silence & Sound — he's losing sound. she's losing time. that's the whole thing, really.
  • Letters — they fall in love in letters. they lose each other, and find each other, in letters.
ch. 01

Strangers

EP 01–10  ·  Autumn

okay so?? this is where it ALL starts and they don't even know each other yet lol. a boy who can't hear and a girl who's scared of her own heart, living in the same city and not knowing it. grab a tissue early, I'm just saying 🤧

#01 🕰️

Tick

📍 Watch shop · dawn · quiet

ok we open on a HUNDRED clocks ticking in the dark and a boy who can only hear half of them because his left ear just... gave up. he winds his grandfather's watch (stopped forever at 6:40, never fixed, we'll come back to this, trust me) and sets it down like it's very special. the SHOP BELL RINGS. he doesn't hear it. nobody's even there yet and I already feel something 😶

“Half a world is still a world.”

✎ first impression

#02 🧣

The Blue Scarf

📍 Khala Sabina's house · morning

meet Vafiya!! she ties her mum's scarf, checks the mirror, counts pills into a tiny tin, and HIDES the bottle behind the rice jar before her aunt walks in. she's already smiling by the time Khala sees her. her mum's heart stopped two months ago. she has the same heart. I'm not okay!!

“I'm fine, Khala. Truly.”

✎ she's hiding something

#03 💌

Ammi's Mailbox

📍 The park bench · dusk (6:40)

she sits on the bench her mum used to sit on and writes her a letter because saying it out loud feels Too Loud. her hand finds a loose slat (a bench board) underneath — just enough space for a folded page. she slides it in. a mailbox. to heaven. okay. I need a minute.

“Dear Ammi, the bench kept your seat warm.”

✎ THE bench (remember this)

#04 🎀

The Letter Under the Slat

📍 The park · dawn (6:40)

Zayn ties his shoe RIGHT at that bench and his fingers brush paper under the slat. he reads a line. then another. looks around like the writer might be hiding behind a tree 😭 slides it back gently and taps twice — I received this. he doesn't even know whose letter it is yet and he's already being so careful with it

“Whoever you are, I hope she hears you.”

✎ the almost-meeting

#05 🚌

Bus No. 7

📍 City bus · morning rush

THEY'RE ON THE SAME BUS and don't know it yet!! she hums her mum's lullaby, his good ear catches the thread of it and for the first time in weeks a sound reaches for him. he turns — bus lurches — doors open — all he gets is a flash of blue scarf stepping into the crowd. NOOO come back

“I know that tune from somewhere I've never been.”

✎ so close??

#06 ☕

Chacha's Chai

📍 Chacha Rahim's tea stall · afternoon

Imran tells a joke, Zayn laughs a beat too late (he didn't actually hear it lol). Chacha Rahim says nothing, just quietly moves the sugar bowl to Zayn's left side, where he'll see it. zero words exchanged. a whole conversation happened anyway. this old man SEES everything 👀

“You're a bad liar, beta.” / “I heard you fine.”

✎ a kind little lie

#07 🏥

The Corridor

📍 City hospital · same hallway

okay THIS one hurt. she walks out of the heart doctor's room — "your heart is tired" — he walks out of the ear doctor's room — "the right ear will follow" — and they pass each other, shoulder to shoulder, eyes down on their own papers. a door closes between them. neither looks up. closest they've ever been and they don't even know it 💔

“Two sentences, two doctors, one hallway.”

✎ ouch. just. ouch

#08 😳

A Reply

📍 The park bench · dusk

she checks the slat expecting her own letter still sitting there and instead there's a REPAIR TAG with handwriting on the back. "Dear Stranger, your Ammi is listening. I'm only half-listening myself, but I'll try. —Morning." she laughs out loud for the first time in TWO MONTHS and then claps a hand over her mouth like she got caught 🥹

“Morning?”

✎ IT BEGINS

#09 🖋️

Two Pens

📍 Split screen: her kitchen / his workbench

she writes, erases, writes again. he does the same thing across town at the exact same time, we LOVE seeing two places at the same time. she signs it Dusk. Khala asks who she's smiling at — "nobody" — Imran asks Zayn the same thing at the same moment — "nobody" — both lying, both SO happy about it 🙈

“Names are heavy. Let's stay light.”

✎ two secret best friends

#10 🌧️

The Rule

📍 The bench · dusk, then dawn

they make a PACT in their letters — no names, no faces, only the bench. just Morning and Dusk, two people who never actually overlap (yeah ok we'll see about that 👀). she tucks the promise under the slat. first raindrop lands on the wood right as the chapter ends. something's coming.

“Only the bench.”

✎ end of ch.1 — a hint of what's coming!!

Letters & Rain

#11 🐦

Small Truths

📍 The park · dawn and dusk, back and forth

their letters go from careful to actually honest. she hates cold tea. he's scared of quiet rooms. she was scared of the dark till 14. he can't whistle (same, honestly). then she asks what his morning sounds like and he holds the pen for AGES before writing one word.

“Birds.” (A lie.)

✎ plant this. it pays off later

#12 ⏱️

Six-Forty

📍 Watch shop · night

he tells Dusk about the grandfather's watch — stopped at 6:40 the day his grandfather died, and he's never been able to fix it. her reply is waiting for him at dawn: "maybe it isn't broken. maybe it's waiting." he just sits on the bench holding the watch, reading it over and over. I AM NOT CRYING

“Maybe it's waiting.”

✎ remember this line too!!

#13 💨

Wind

📍 Main road · windy afternoon

the wind just PULLS her scarf off her neck and she runs after it through traffic laughing — then not laughing. her chest tightens, steps slow down, hand goes to her ribs. the scarf rises over the rooftops and is just. gone. she sits on the curb and breathes. and breathes.

“Not that. Anything but that.”

✎ scary moment

#14 🔔

The Shop Bell

📍 Watch shop · afternoon

the scarf drifted into an OPEN SHOP DOOR and landed on a workbench and she follows it in. the bell rings right on his deaf side — he doesn't turn. she waits, coughs, taps the counter. he finally looks up startled and. there it is. they see each other's faces for the first time ever and don't even know who the other person IS yet

“Is this yours?”

✎ THEY MET. kind of. aww

#15 🙊

"Didn't You Hear Me?"

📍 Watch shop · same moment

she's annoyed — "didn't you hear the bell?! I stood here for a full minute!" — and he just touches his ear. "I... don't. on this side." her anger fades into embarrassment, she mumbles thanks and rushes out. that night she tells Khala about "a rude boy at the watch shop" and can't stop smiling while she says it 🙈 oh, girl.

“Sorry. My left ear...”

✎ oops. she felt bad

#16 🪑

Opposite Ends

📍 The bench · dusk

Zayn closes the shop early hoping to catch the letter-writer. Vafiya shows up for the SAME reason. each finds a total stranger already sitting there ("the rude girl" / "the boy from the shop") and they sit stiffly at opposite ends pretending to read. the loose slat sits right between them and NEITHER ONE REACHES FOR IT. I am going insane

“Silence, two metres of wood, and a secret in the middle.”

✎ it's so unfair, and it's KILLING me

#17 ☔

One Umbrella

📍 The park path · rain

he offers his umbrella, she says no, then yes a beat later (classic). they walk to the bus stop together, she remembers to face him so he can read her lips. names FINALLY happen — "I'm Vafiya." "Zayn." — and then she hums without thinking and he goes very still. "that tune... bus 7." oh he KNOWS

“You ride 7?”

✎ the pieces are connecting!!

#18 💧

Drowned Words

📍 The bench · next morning and evening

the rain soaked the letters under the slat, ink bleeding into blue rivers. he finds it first, then she does at dusk, holding the wet paper like it's something wounded. neither knows the other is sad about the exact same thing at the exact same bench. he rewrites it all from memory.

“The rain washed your words away. I kept every one.”

✎ he remembered every word 🥺

#19 🎧

Feel the Tick

📍 Watch shop · afternoon

she comes back with tea as an apology. he shows her the pocket watch. she asks him to wind it then holds it gently against his jaw. "listen." through bone, through skull, a faint tick reaches him — and Zayn, who hasn't heard a watch in two years, cries without making a single sound. I'm actually sobbing

“Hold it to your jaw. Now listen.”

✎ PEAK. a true peak episode

#20 ✍️

The Same Handwriting

📍 Watch shop · same moment

while he's wiping his eyes she glances at a repair tag on the counter. the loop of the y. the slant of the M. she goes very still. she looks at his hands, the string, the brown paper. it was never a stranger under the slat. it was HIM this whole time. she says nothing. just smiles. and leaves with her heart doing something it is absolutely not allowed to do 😭

“Morning.” (a whisper, to no one)

✎ end of ch.2 — SHE KNOWS!!!

The Test

📍 The bench · dusk, then dawn

she doesn't tell him who she is yet — writes one more letter: "tell me honestly, what does your morning sound like?" then HIDES behind the tea stall at dawn to watch him read it. this is a whole plan. she sits very still watching him sit very still. so tense!!

“Tell me honestly.”

✎ she's testing him

#22 🤫

Silence

📍 The bench · dusk

his letter back: "Silence, Dusk. It has been getting louder than any sound I ever heard." she reads it and the whole park just goes quiet around her. NOW she understands — the shop boy, the bell, the sugar bowl, the lie about birds. it all clicks. she cries, wipes her eyes, and decides something.

“Silence, Dusk.”

✎ everything clicks into place

#23 🫂

It's You

📍 Watch shop · morning

SHE RINGS THE BELL KNOWING HE CAN'T HEAR IT and just walks right up to where he works so he'll see her. puts down the scarf, a letter, a pen. he reads the signature — Dusk. looks up. time just. stops. then they're laughing and crying at the same time and I am SO not okay rn

“Hello, Morning.” / “Hello, Dusk.”

✎ ⭐ THE BIG MOMENT ⭐ they find out!!!

#24 🍂

The First Walk

📍 Park path · afternoon

now that names FIT, everything is just easier. she turns her face toward him when she talks so he can read her lips. he teaches her to count seconds by watch ticks. they get lost in the old part of the park and don't mind one bit. she tells him about her mum for the first time and he doesn't say "I'm sorry" — just walks slower. he just WALKS SLOWER you guys

“You don't have to say anything. I'll walk slower.”

✎ this man did everything right

#25 👆

Three Taps

📍 The bench · dusk

in a crowd, in the dark, in fog — he can't always hear her. so they invent a sign: three taps on the wrist. "I'm here." they practice it ten times and giggle every single time. the simplest language in the world and somehow the most important one in this whole story (remember THIS one especially)

Tap. Tap. Tap. “I'm here.”

✎ 🔑 this comes back SO many times

#26 🫖

Two Cups

📍 Chacha Rahim's stall · evening

Imran nearly drops his tea when Zayn shows up with a GIRL. talks for four minutes without stopping. Chacha Rahim says nothing but sets down two cups — no sugar for her, extra for him — exactly right without even asking. when they leave he mutters to the kettle, "finally." old man knew all along 😌

“It's the first time you've ordered two.”

✎ side characters being sweet

#27 🪁

The Kite

📍 The hills · windy afternoon

he built her a kite from watch-shop paper and thin wire! she runs with it laughing, blue scarf streaming behind her, up the slope — until her steps get shorter and her hand flies to her chest. she turns it into a joke "I'm tired! you hold it!" but his eyes don't leave her hand on her chest. he noticed.

“I'm tired! You hold it!”

✎ a small warning sign

#28 💊

The Pill

📍 The bench · dusk

her bag tips, the pill tin rolls under the bench. he picks it up, reads the label. she takes it back gently — "a weak heart. a small thing. everyone has something." he nods and wants so badly to believe her. she makes him promise not to fuss. he promises. and the promise just sits in his chest like a swallowed stone 😔

“Everyone has something.”

✎ he's carrying it quietly now

#29 🎡

The Ferris Wheel

📍 The night fair

at the very top, the whole town is just a handful of lights. she says something quietly and turns away. "what did you say?" she turns back and mouths it slowly so he can read every shape. he reads it. his whole face changes. okay I'm screaming a little

“I... like... you.”

✎ 🎡 she says she likes him!! finally!!

#30 💡

The Streetlamp

#31 🎵

Hum

📍 The bench · dusk

he asks her to hum the lullaby again and asks if he can rest his fingertips on her throat while she does. she hums. for the first time he feels a song as a small trembling, rising and falling like breath. eyes closed. sound isn't gone, he realizes — it just lives somewhere else now.

“Again. Slower. I want to learn it by touch.”

✎ so so soft

#32 🎸

The Dusty Guitar

📍 Zayn's attic · afternoon

under a sheet — a guitar. she runs a finger over the strings, looks up. "play." he shakes his head. "a guitarist who can't hear is a joke." she goes quiet for a second, then: "then I'll be your ears." he's not ready yet. she lets it go gently but tucks the sheet back more carefully than before, like putting something sleeping back to bed 🥺

“A musician is someone who plays. Not someone who hears.”

✎ not yet, but soon...

#33 🍛

Khala's Biryani

Khala asks him lots of questions (kindly) while he reads her lips for every question. she watches him cut Vafiya's meat without even being asked. approved by dessert. in the kitchen she grabs his sleeve: "be gentle with her heart." he doesn't yet know how for real she means that line 😢

“Be gentle with her heart.”

#34 ⚙️

Gears

#35 🧣

📍 The bench · cold evening

#36 📓

Before I'm Old

she shows him a little notebook — "before I'm old." see the sea. dance without music. write a hundred letters. eat mango in the rain. he reads quietly, then takes the pen and adds his OWN line: "Zayn: hear the sea." she stares at it. "you can't." "then I'll feel it." I'm... okay

#37 🎶

The Music Box

📍 Watch shop · night

he's been building something for weeks — gears from five dead watches, a brass comb, a tiny hand-punched cylinder. winds it, sets it in her hands. her mother's lullaby, playing in small bright notes SHE can hear and HE cannot. she weeps. he puts his hand over hers on the box and just feels it hum. I need a tissue. several tissues.

“I can't hear it. But I can feel you hearing it.”

#38 🌫️

Fog

“Tap. Tap. Tap.”

✎ scary for a second, then relief

Fixed

Night Train

#53 🎐

What the Sea Sounds Like

📍 Wooden pier · morning

"I can't hear it," he says gently. she thinks, then lays him flat on the pier. the boards hum. each wave arrives through the wood and into his ribs — a slow boom, a hiss, a long pause. "that," she whispers, "that's what it sounds like." he closes his eyes. he HEARS the sea. not with ears. with her.

“Press your ear to the wood.”
